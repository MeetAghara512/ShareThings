ShareThings

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

ShareThings is a one-to-one video calling and screen sharing service. This page explains what information the service handles and why. In short: calls are not recorded, there are no user accounts in a database, and there is no advertising or analytics.

Information we handle

  • Display name. The name you type before joining a room is saved in your browser and sent to the other person in your call so they can see who you are.
  • Google sign-in (optional). If you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address from Google. Our server checks the sign-in token and keeps your name and email in memory only while you are connected. The sign-in token is saved in your browser so you stay signed in; it expires after about one hour.
  • Email invitations. When a signed-in user sends an invitation, we use the guest's email address to send that one email. The email shows the name and email address of the person who sent it. To prevent abuse, the sender's email, the guest's email and the sender's IP address are kept in the server's memory for up to 24 hours and then discarded. Separately, the email service that delivers the invitation keeps its own record of sent messages, including the guest's address.
  • Call audio, video and screen. Media goes directly between the two browsers whenever possible. When a direct connection is not possible it passes through a relay server, encrypted. We do not record, store or look at your calls.
  • Connection data. To set up a call, the room ID and technical connection details, including IP addresses, pass through our server and are shared with the other person's browser. This is how browser-to-browser calling works. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or rent your information.
  • We do not use advertising or analytics trackers.
  • We do not keep a database of users, calls or invitations.
  • We do not use your Google account for anything other than confirming who you are when you send invitations.

Use of Google user data

ShareThings uses Google sign-in only to confirm the name and email address of a user who wants to send invitations. That information is shown to the invited guest and is not used for any other purpose, shared with anyone else, or stored after you disconnect. Invitation emails are sent from the ShareThings mailbox, not from your Google account.

Services we rely on

  • Google: sign-in, and helping browsers find a direct connection.
  • Brevo: delivery of invitation emails.
  • Xirsys: relay servers for calls that cannot connect directly.
  • Render: hosting for the website and the server.

Your choices

  • You can use ShareThings without signing in.
  • Signing out removes the sign-in token from your browser.
  • Clearing this site's data in your browser removes your saved display name and sign-in token.
  • If you received an invitation you did not want, write to the address below and we will look into it.
